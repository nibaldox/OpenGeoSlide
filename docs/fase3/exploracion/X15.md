- **[verificado]** «Various types of slope reinforcement can be modelled in *Slide2*, including: End Anchored support … Geosynthetics … Grouted Tiebacks / Ground Anchors … Piles / Micro Piles … Soil Nails … RSPile … Launched Soil Nails … User-defined support … Retaining Wall (EFP) … Helical Anchor». https://www.rocscience.com/help/slide2/documentation/slide-model/support-2
- **[verificado]** Lista de tipos predefinidos: «End Anchored, Geosynthetic, Grouted Tieback, Grouted Tieback With Friction, Micro Pile, Soil Nail, Launched Soil Nail, RSPile, Helical Anchor, Retaining Wall (EFP)»; «User-defined … if the pre-defined support types … are not adequate». https://www.rocscience.com/help/slide2/documentation/slide-model/support-2/define-support-properties
- **[verificado]** Regla de intersección: «in order for the support to have an effect on a given slip surface, the support must intersect the slip surface. If the support does NOT intersect a slip surface, then NO support force will be applied». https://www.rocscience.com/help/slide2/documentation/slide-model/support-2/define-support-properties/overview-of-support-implementation
- **[verificado]** Ubicación: «a force is applied at the point of intersection of the slip surface with the support (i.e. to the base of a single slice). The applied force is simply a line load, with units of FORCE per unit width of slope.» (misma URL).

### 1.2 Parámetros y forma de la fuerza disponible

- **[verificado]** Diagrama de fuerza: «For End Anchored or Pile support, the Force Diagram is simply a horizontal line»; «For Geosynthetic, Grouted Tieback, Soil Nail, and Helical Anchor support … The Failure Mode which generates the MINIMUM force determines the support force». Para tiebacks y clavos se añaden modos de cizalla y compresión (misma URL).
- **[verificado]** Clavo (https://www.rocscience.com/help/slide2/documentation/slide-model/support-2/define-support-properties/soil-nail): «Tensile Capacity … the capacity of the soil nail itself (e.g. steel tensile capacity), independent of the plate capacity or the bond capacity»; «Plate Capacity … the maximum load which can be sustained by the plate assembly»; «Shear Capacity is optional»; «Compression Capacity is optional»; «Out of Plane Spacing … center to center»; «For a Soil Nail, the Pullout Strength is expressed as a Force per unit Length»; «A Soil Nail is assumed to be fully bonded along its entire length»; «a Grouted Tieback with Bond Length = 100% would behave exactly the same as a Soil Nail».
- **[verificado]** Clavo, fuerza mínima: «At any point along the length of a soil nail, there are 3 possible failure modes … 1. Pullout … 2. Tensile Failure … 3. Stripping»; «Applied Force = min (F1, F2, F3)». «In order for stripping to occur, the Plate Capacity must be exceeded»; si «Plate Capacity ≥ Tensile Capacity, … stripping cannot occur» (misma URL).
- **[verificado]** Clavo con cizalla: «an additional force vector perpendicular to the bolt direction … is added to the overall bolt capacity vector» (misma URL).
- **[verificado]** Tieback, ejemplo de entrada del tutorial de Slide: «Enter Bonded Length (percent) = 50, and Bond Strength = 15» (https://www.rocscience.com/downloads/slide/Slide_TutorialManual.pdf).
- **[verificado]** Geosintéticos, interacción: «Adhesion = cohesion*Ci … Friction angle = arctan(Ci*tan(phi))» (https://www.rocscience.com/help/slide2/documentation/knowledge-base/reinforcement).
- **[verificado]** Ejemplo de la guía de geosintéticos de Rocscience: «Adhesion = 0,8 x c´FILL = 0,8 x 10 kPa = 8 kPa; Friction angle = atan (0,8 x tan φ´FILL) = atan (0,8 x tan 28°) = 23°» (https://static.rocscience.cloud/assets/verification-and-theory/Slide2/guideline_on_using_geosynthetics_in_slide.pdf; `slide_geosynth.txt` l. 201–203).
- **[verificado]** Pilotes: «For Piles / Micro Piles, the orientation of the support force is tangent to the slip surface, perpendicular to the pile, or horizontal (in the EFW case).» (URL overview-of-support-implementation).
- **[verificado]** Pilote como resistencia al corte: «If you use a material layer … 0.2 m … 60kN of shear … This is the same as defining a micropile with a shear strength of 60kN and a spacing = 1 meter» (https://www.rocscience.com/help/slide2/documentation/knowledge-base/reinforcement).

### 1.3 Activo / pasivo y dirección

- **[verificado]** «active support forces work to reduce the driving forces in the factor of safety calculations, while passive support forces work to increase the resisting forces» (knowledge-base/reinforcement).
- **[verificado]** Activo (Método A, Duncan & Wright): «the support force is NOT divided by the factor of safety … the support capacity input by the user for Active (Method A) should be the ALLOWABLE support force». Pasivo (Método B): «the support forces ARE divided by the factor of safety … should be the ULTIMATE support force» (misma URL).
- **[verificado]** «In terms of convergence properties, it has been found that active support is generally better.» (misma URL).
- **[verificado]** Orientación: «For End Anchored support, Grouted Tiebacks, Soil Nails, and Geosynthetics or User-Defined support, the support force can be applied tangent to the slip surface, parallel to the support, at an angle which bisects the tangent and parallel angles, or at any user-defined angle.» (URL overview-of-support-implementation).
- **[verificado]** Sin pretensado: «There is no way to put a pre-/post-tensioning force into Slide2.» (knowledge-base/reinforcement).
- **[verificado]** Cizalla + tracción en clavos: «Slide2 computes a support force vector for tensile failure and for shear failure independently … It then adds the tensile force vector to the shear force vector» (misma URL).

### 1.4 Combinación tracción, arrancamiento y placa

- Tracción (`Tensile Capacity`): tope de la barra; independiente de placa y bulbo.
- Arrancamiento (`Pullout`, fuerza por unidad de longitud): modo de fallo en la longitud embebida más allá de la superficie.
- Placa (`Plate Capacity`): solo limita el modo de «stripping», donde la superficie deja la barra embebida.
- La fuerza aplicada es el mínimo de los modos en cada punto (cita de §1.2).

- **[verificado]** Resistencia del muro: «If the mode of failure is assumed to cause rupture of the wall itself, then you can use the shear strength of the wall material … If failure is assumed to create a passive type bearing failure of the toe material, then this strength should be used.» (misma URL).

### 2.4 Qué no cubre el equilibrio límite

- **[verificado]** «For anchored sheet pile walls, the classical method of determining depth of penetration can be done using limit equilibrium … However, moment equilibrium does not consider stiffness of the wall, and the soil which affect the performance of the sheet-pile wall. … The location of maximum moment does not coincide with limit equilibrium.» (Wong, K. F., NTU, 2009: https://dr.ntu.edu.sg/handle/10356/15883).
- **[verificado]** «Conventional methods used in the design of sheet pile walls are based on force and moment equilibrium using active and passive earth pressures that are concerned with the failure condition.» (https://igs.org.in/storage/proceedings-uploads/TH10-44-180523121335.pdf).
- **[verificado]** «Although the excavation depth may be increased thanks to the existence of the anchor, it should not be forgotten, that until the anchor is placed, the structure behaves as a cantilever sheet pile wall.» (misma URL).

Conclusión §2: el equilibrio límite de talud (Slide2, Plaxis LE) verifica la estabilidad global con la tablestaca como resistencia de corte. El empuje, el empotramiento, el momento y la deflexión son de cálculo de muro (GEO5 Sheeting, Plaxis FEM); el talud no los calcula [inferido].

## 3. Geosintéticos y muros TEM

### 3.1 Resistencia de diseño a largo plazo (FHWA)

- **[verificado]** FHWA-NHI-10-024 (GEC 11, vol. I): «The available long-term strength, Tal, is calculated as follows … Tult = Ultimate Tensile Strength (strength per unit width) … RF = Reduction Factor. The product of all applicable reduction factors.» (https://highways.dot.gov/media/56196).
- **[verificado]** FHWA-NHI-10-025 (vol. II): «Allowable geosynthetic strength (Eq. 3-12), Tal = ultimate strength (TULT) ÷ reduction factor (RF) for creep, installation damage and durability» (https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/engineering/geotech/pubs/nhi10025/nhi10025.pdf).
- **[verificado]** RFID: «A minimum reduction factor of 1.1 should be used to account for testing uncertainties.» (https://highways.dot.gov/media/56196).
- **[verificado]** RFCR: «The creep reduction factor is the ratio of the ultimate load to the extrapolated maximum sustainable load (i.e., creep rupture limit) within the design life of the structure (e.g., several years for temporary structures, 75 to 100 years for permanent structures).» (misma URL).
- **[verificado]** RFD: «typically varies from 1.1 to 2.0» (https://highways.dot.gov/media/206831, la revisión de GEC 11 de 2024; «a revision and an update to the FHWA NHI-10-024»).
- **[verificado]** Durabilidad mínima en vol. I: «the long-term nominal strength may be calculated with a minimum durability reduction factor of 1.0 in place of 1.1 minimum used for permanent walls» (https://highways.dot.gov/media/56196; misma fuente en PennDOT copia).
- **[verificado]** Ejemplo de factores (FHWA-HIF-17-004, §7.4 y cap. 9): «The reduction factor for durability, RFd, and for installation damage, RFid, are each taken as 1.1. The reduction factor for creep, RFc is 1.66. Hence … RF = 1.1×1.1×1.66 ≅ 2.0.» (https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/engineering/geotech/pubs/hif17004.pdf).
- **[verificado]** «For global stability, the long term design strength, LTDS, of the reinforcement is needed.» (misma fuente).
- **[verificado]** NHI-10-025, ejemplo: «RFCR = creep reduction factor = 3.0» (snippet de búsqueda; contexto del ejemplo [pendiente], no abierto en el PDF).

### 3.2 BS 8006

- **[verificado]** Fragmento de la fórmula de diseño ULS de BS 8006 usado por un fabricante: «TD … Tchar/RFCS … ƒm = partial material factor … RFID x RFW x RFCH x fs» (https://www.fibertex.com/sites/fibertex.com/files/2022-01/Secugrid%20R6%20Long_term_design_strength.pdf). - **[verificado]** Otra fuente dice otra cosa: «BS 8006-1:2010 restricts the maximum creep strain over the design life to 2%.» (https://library.geosyntheticssociety.org/wp-content/uploads/resources/proceedings/122021/S33-03.pdf). **Discrepancia**: 0.5 % / 1.0 % frente a 2 %. ### 3.3 Arrancamiento con coeficiente de interacción

- **[verificado]** FHWA-NHI-10-024 (https://highways.dot.gov/media/206831): «For geosynthetic (i.e., geogrid and geotextile) sheet reinforcement, the pullout resistance is based on reducing the available soil friction with the reduction factor often referred to as the Coefficient of Interaction, Ci.»

### 4.1 Adherencia del bulbo y arrancamiento

- **[verificado]** FHWA-NHI-14-007 (GEC 7), §4.5.6b: «Pullout Resistance per Unit Length». Texto del manual: «The ultimate pullout resistance per unit length of soil nail (rPO) is equal to the bond strength multiplied by the perimeter of the bond zone.» (https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/engineering/geotech/pubs/nhi14007.pdf; `nhi14007.txt`).
- **[verificado]** Fórmula: «nominal pullout resistance (per unit length) of soil nail = π × qu × DDH» (misma fuente; ecuaciones 9.1–9.2 de longitud de bulbo de verificación).
- **[verificado]** Valores presuntos (Tabla 4.6, modificada de Sabatini et al. 1999, en kip/ft): arena y grava densa: 20; arena media: 10; arena densa: 13; arcilla–limo firme: 2; firme a dura: 4 (`nhi14007.txt`, Tabla 4.6).
- **[verificado]** Verificación: «The unbonded length shall be at least 3 ft within the drill hole» (nhi14007, pruebas de verificación).
- **[verificado]** FHWA-IF-99-015 (GEC 4): «The maximum allowable anchor design load in soil may be determined by multiplying the bond length by the ultimate transfer load and dividing by a factor of safety of 2.0.» (https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/engineering/geotech/pubs/if99015.pdf; `if99015.txt` l. 3453–3455).
- **[verificado]** Tabla 6 de IF-99-015 (valores presuntivos de transferencia de carga, kN/m): arena y grava: 145 (suelta), 220 (media), 290 (densa); arena: 100, 145, 190; arena y limo: 70, 100, 130; arcilla–limo: 30 (firme), 60 (dura) (`if99015.txt` l. 3460–3475).
- **[verificado]** Ejemplo numérico de IF-99-015 (anexo, hoja GEOSYNTEC): «The design load with a factor of safety of 2.0 should be able to be achieved with a typical soil anchor bond length of 12 m … the bond strength is [(100kN/m)/2.0]x12 m = 600 kN. The allowable anchor capacity of 600 kN is larger than the maximum design load of 445 kN.» (`if99015.txt` l. 8822–8826).
- **[verificado]** Longitud libre mínima: «The minimum unbonded length for rock and soil ground anchors is 4.5 m for strand tendons and 3 m for bar tendons.» (https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/engineering/geotech/pubs/if99015.pdf, búsqueda).

### 4.2 Longitud libre y longitud de bulbo: resumen
- Tirante activo: longitud libre ≥ 4.5 m (cordón) o 3 m (barra) [verificado, IF-99-015].
- Clavo: en pruebas, longitud libre ≥ 3 ft (≈ 0.9 m) [verificado, NHI-14-007].
- Sin límite de bulbo en Slide2: Slide2 usa `Bond Strength` por unidad de longitud y `Bond Length` como porcentaje (tutorial, §1.2). Las longitudes de bulbo entran en el cálculo de la fuerza en la superficie.

## 5. Recomendación: conjunto mínimo

### 5.1 Conjunto mínimo (cuatro tipos)

1. **`anchor`** (tirante postensado o pasivo con bulbo):
   - `tensileCapacity`, `bond` (fuerza por unidad de longitud × longitud de bulbo, o porcentaje como Slide2), `spacing`, `application` (`active`/`passive`), `direction` (`axial`/`tangent`/`bisector`).
   - Fuentes: Slide2 (tiebacks VP 58, 59, 85, 86); IF-99-015 (longitud libre mínima).
2. **`nail`** (clavo de suelo):
   - `tensileCapacity`, `plate` (tope de la placa), `bond` (fuerza por longitud), `spacing`, `application`, `direction`.
   - Cizalla y compresión: fuera del mínimo (opcional en Slide2).
   - Fuentes: VP 47, 48, 50, 60; NHI-14-007 (Tabla 4.6; π·qu·DDH).
3. **`geosynthetic`**:
   - `tensileCapacity` (Tal ya reducido, o Tult con `RF`), `adhesion`/`frictionAngle` (o `Ci`), `coverage` (100 % por defecto), `anchorage` (ambos extremos), `application`, `direction` (tangente o bisectriz).
   - Fuentes: VP 30–32, 39, 87–94, 107; NHI-10-024/025 (Tal = Tult/RF); HIF-17-004 (Ci).
4. **`pile`** (pilote o micropilote; `sheet_pile` como `pile` con `wall: true`):
   - `shearCapacity` (kN por unidad de muro o por pilote), `spacing`, `direction` (`tangent`, `perpendicular`, `horizontal`), `force` (constante) y `location`.
   - Fuentes: VP 54, 106, 49 (micropilote); KB Slide2 (tablestaca como micropilote con espaciamiento 1 m).

### 5.2 Cobertura

- **[verificado]** Los casos de soporte del manual de Slide2 (§1.5) usan estos cuatro tipos: tiebacks (49, 58, 59, 85, 86), clavos (47, 48, 50, 60), geosintéticos (30, 31, 32, 39, 87–94, 107), pilotes (49, 54, 106). Quedan fuera solo 110 (EFP, bloqueado) y 111 (helicoidal, sin talud).
- **[inferido]** Esto no prueba el «90 % de los usos en minería y obras civiles en Chile y LatAm». Ninguna fuente de este informe cuantifica el uso local. La cobertura del 90 % es una estimación a validar con los casos reales del equipo.

### 5.3 Posponer

- **Pretensado/postensado**: Slide2 no lo tiene (KB: «no way to put a pre-/post-tensioning force into Slide2»); cSlope sí (`prestress` en `Reinforcement`, `__init__.pyi` l. 467) [verificado]. Decidir antes de congelar `anchor`.
- **Clavos lanzados** (`Launched Soil Nail`, RSPile) y **helicoidales** (`Helical Anchor`; VP 111 no tiene talud): fuera del mínimo.
- **Retaining wall (EFP)**: VP 110 bloqueado; fuera del mínimo.
- **Pullout por materiales** (`Material Dependent`, KB Slide2): fuera del mínimo, se usa un valor único por refuerzo.
- **Cizalla y compresión de clavos**: opcionales (Slide2 las marca como optional).
- **Tablestaca como muro de flexión** (momento, deflexión, empotramiento): fuera del equilibrio límite; sin modelo en Slide2, GEO5 da utilizaciones y Plaxis LE usa micropilote (§2.3–2.4).
- **Soporte definido por el usuario** (`User-Defined`, Slide2): se puede aproximar con `pile`/`anchor`; posponer.
